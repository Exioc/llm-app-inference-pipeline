## Description
**Avast Cleanup – Smart Phone Cleaner & Storage Manager**   
  
Avast Cleanup is a powerful phone cleaner and storage manager designed to help you remove junk files and perform deep cleaning on your Android device for an organized experience. With advanced cleaning tools and smart cleaning technology, Avast Cleanup turns your device into a cleaner phone. Whether you need to clean your phone, manage storage space, remove junk, or identify what drains your battery, Avast Cleanup delivers a complete cleaning experience.  
  
Go beyond simple storage management by identifying hidden, unnecessary files that slow down your device. Our advanced cleaning technology detects leftover data and temporary files from uninstalled apps that occupy valuable storage. By removing these digital leftovers, you get more than a better organized phone — you reclaim space for new photos, videos, and important applications.   
  
Get more from your device with our intelligent cleaning features. Avast Cleanup doesn't just delete files; App Analyzer helps you identify apps that are active in the background, so you can manage them more easily.   
  
Organize your media and gallery with our smart photo cleaning feature. We help you identify blurry, poor-quality, or duplicate photos. From managing large files to organizing your downloads, Avast Cleanup is the all-in-one app to keep your phone clean.
